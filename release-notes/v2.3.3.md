## Highlights

Elements get a Modern style with lighter outlines and a custom dropdown. Classic, the original look, stays available.

## What's new

- Modern style for Elements (Enterprise, `elements`): lighter 1px outlines, softer focus rings, tappable extras, wrapping option buttons, a calmer result card, and a custom dropdown with a rounded panel, a tinted selected row and a check mark. The dropdown works with a mouse, a touch screen and the keyboard, and the native select stays underneath as a fallback.
- Choose the style under Assets > Element templates > Edit theme. New workspaces start on Modern. Workspaces that already saved a theme stay on Classic until they switch, so live pages do not change on their own.
- Both styles follow your theme colours, radius and font.

## Fixes and improvements

- General stability and performance improvements.
