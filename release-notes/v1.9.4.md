# v1.9.4: layout and accuracy fixes found on a live review

A pass over the live pages after v1.9.3 turned up these.

## Layout

- **Home tiles.** While an audit ran, the "SEO issues" tile showed its
  progress on a second line and became taller than the three tiles
  beside it. The progress now sits inside the tile's row as a small
  badge ("160 of 1,414" with a pulsing dot), so all four tiles keep the
  same height. The full wording is in the tooltip and is read out by
  screen readers.
- **Workspace settings.** The site and crawler fields were a ragged
  two-column grid with one field left alone on a row and two long
  paragraphs of help. They are now grouped: Site URL and Time zone
  together, then an "SEO crawler" block with the two rates and the
  re-check window in one row, the audit fetch prefix across the full
  width, and a short hint under each group.
- **Progress banners.** "Audit in progress" on the Optimizer and the
  Issues Snapshot was tinted with the brand colour, which is red in
  some workspaces and read as an error. It is now a neutral grey.
- **SEO Health card.** The caption under the counts is one short line
  ("Audit in progress, N of M" during a run) instead of three.
- **PageSpeed connector card.** Shorter description, so the card
  matches its neighbours.
- The "set your site URL" link now opens the System tab directly.

## Accuracy

- **"No Article schema" on service pages.** A content type left on the
  default page type counted as article content, so pages with Service
  or FAQ schema were reported as missing Article schema (825 on the
  workspace reviewed). The finding is now raised only when a page has
  no schema that describes it: an article page with nothing but
  generic blocks such as BreadcrumbList or Organization.
- **Issues showing "0".** Site-wide findings with no page list, such
  as slow LCP or unminified CSS, were listed with "0 issues" and added
  nothing to the total. Each now counts as one issue.

## Notes

No migrations, no API route changes, no config changes. Issue totals
will change after upgrading: fewer schema findings, and one more for
each site-wide performance finding.
